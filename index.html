🛡️ Ogshop Tech & Security

Web2-to-Web3 Seamless Gateway: We utilize Embedded Hidden Wallet technology. Users do not need complex knowledge of Seed Phrases or Private Keys. Your Gmail account acts as an intelligent key, making the Web3 shopping experience as simple as traditional e-commerce.
Self-Custody Ownership: Every wallet is generated through a secure hashing algorithm combining your Email and personal Password. Ogshop strictly does not store your keys. You have full control over your assets, ensuring maximum security and non-custodial access.
0G Distributed Storage System: Order data is not stored on hackable centralized servers. We encrypt and distribute data across the global 0G Storage network. This guarantees absolute privacy, permanent accessibility, and protection against unauthorized deletion or tampering.
On-chain Transparency: All transactions are executed on the 0G Mainnet. 100% transparency is ensured via the public Ledger, providing both buyers and sellers with indisputable proof of transaction.
